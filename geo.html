The shift

Patients are starting their search with AI

A growing share of searches now return an AI-written answer before any normal results. Google shows AI Overviews at the top of many searches, and more people are asking assistants like ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot and Perplexity direct questions such as "who's a good implant dentist near me?" or "how much is Invisalign in Sheffield?".

Those answers usually name only a handful of practices, and the patient often never sees a list of ten blue links. If your practice isn't one of the names the AI is confident about, you can be invisible for exactly the high-intent questions that lead to bookings, even if you rank well in normal search.

GEO is how you improve those odds. It isn't a trick or a hack. It's making sure the facts about your practice are clear, consistent and backed up across the web, so AI tools can use them with confidence.

Questions

GEO & AI search, FAQs

What is GEO?

GEO (generative engine optimisation) is the work of making your practice easy for AI assistants to understand, trust and recommend. When a patient asks ChatGPT, Google's AI Overviews, Gemini, Copilot or Perplexity for a dentist or a treatment, GEO improves the chances your practice is the one named.

Is GEO different from SEO?

It builds on the same foundations, such as a fast, well-structured website, a strong Google Business Profile and genuine reviews, but it focuses on how AI tools gather and summarise information: clear answers, consistent facts about your practice across the web, structured data and trusted third-party mentions.

Can you guarantee my practice will appear in ChatGPT or AI Overviews?

No. Nobody controls what AI assistants say, and promising specific placements would breach advertising guidance. We commit to the work, track how often your practice is mentioned for the questions that matter, and report honestly on what changes.

How much does it cost?

GEO starts from £450 per month. Many practices run it alongside our dental SEO, as the two reinforce each other. We'll recommend the right scope after a free audit.

How long does it take to see a difference?

Some fixes, like correcting inconsistent practice details or adding structured data, can be picked up quickly. Being cited more often by AI assistants usually builds over several months, in a similar way to SEO.

Is GEO compliant with GDC and ASA rules?

It should be, and we build it that way. AI tools repeat what they find about you, so we make sure the information we publish is accurate, avoids misleading claims and doesn't rely on patient testimonials in ways the rules don't allow.
