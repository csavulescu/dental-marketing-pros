Why it matters

Most dental marketing isn't compliant

Research has repeatedly found that compliance with current GDC and ASA advertising guidance is generally poor across dental practices. Patient testimonials, unsubstantiated claims and "guaranteed results" language are common, and increasingly risky as enforcement tightens.

We handle the marketing that grows your practice and keeps you on the right side of the rules.

Compliance built into everything

We design campaigns and content with the guidelines in mind from the start, so you're not left exposed:

  • No patient testimonials on patient-facing pages (prohibited in dental advertising)
  • No "guaranteed" outcomes or unjustified expectations
  • Clear NHS / private / mixed disclosure where required
  • GDC registration details and required website information displayed
  • Before/after imagery handled with proper consent and no misleading edits
