Questions

AI websites, FAQs

Is there really no upfront cost?

Yes. There's nothing to pay upfront. You pay £50 per month, which covers the build, hosting, your domain, SSL, backups and content changes.

How long is the contract?

The minimum term is 12 months. After that it rolls on monthly, and you can cancel at any time by giving 30 days' notice.

What does "built with AI" mean?

We use AI tools to design and build your site to your requirements, which is what keeps it fast to produce and affordable. It's still a managed service: we take your brief, build the site, make the changes you need and look after it once it's live.

What kind of websites do you build?

Presentation websites that showcase your practice, team and treatments, and lead-generation websites designed to turn visitors into enquiries and bookings. We don't build ecommerce or online shop websites.

Can I make changes after the site is live?

Yes. Content changes are included, so if you need to update text, images, opening hours or a treatment page, just send us the details and we'll make the change for you.

How is this different from your web design service?

Our web design service is a bespoke, fully custom build from £150/month. AI websites are a faster, lower-cost option for practices that want a professional presentation or lead-generation site without a bespoke build.
