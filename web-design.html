Why dental website design matters more than it looks

Your website is often a patient's first impression of your dental practice, long before they meet your team. A dated or slow site makes a private practice look like a risk, while a professionally designed website builds credibility before the phone rings.

Many dental websites are built on generic templates. They are quick to launch, but they tend to look like every other practice in town and rarely have the treatment pages or tracking a growing practice needs. Our dental website designs start from your patients and your local market, then use proven layouts so the user experience stays simple on every device.

Good design also supports your wider online presence. A clear structure helps search engines understand your treatments and locations, and the same pages work as landing pages for dental Google Ads. If you are deciding between platforms, our guide to WordPress vs a custom build covers the trade-offs.

Signs your dental website needs a redesign…

  • It is hard to use on a phone
  • Pages take more than a few seconds to load
  • Treatments have no pages of their own
  • Patients can't book or enquire online
  • You can't see which pages bring enquiries

Questions

Dental website design: frequently asked questions

What does £150/month include?

Design, build, content, an SEO-ready structure, hosting and support for the 12-month term. You get a complete, professional website with no large upfront cost.

What happens after the 12 months?

The site is up and running and we'll talk you through your options for continuing hosting, support and any ongoing improvements. We'll always be upfront about what comes next.

How long until my site is live?

Typically 4–6 weeks from kick-off. The biggest factor is how quickly we receive your content, images and feedback, we'll keep things moving from our side.

Will it work with my SEO and ads?

Absolutely, that's the point. The site is built SEO-ready, so it gives your SEO and PPC campaigns a strong, conversion-focused foundation.

Is the content compliant with GDC and ASA rules?

Yes. We build in the required disclosures and registration details and avoid non-compliant patient testimonials and unsubstantiated claims, so your site keeps you on the right side of the rules.

Can I update the site myself?

We'll discuss your preferences during discovery. Many practices prefer us to handle updates as part of support, but we'll set things up to suit how you want to work.

How much does a dental website cost?

Our dental web design starts from £150/month over 12 months, which includes design, build, content, hosting and support for the term. Larger websites with lots of pages or custom features are quoted individually, and we always confirm the price before we start.

How many pages should a dental website have?

Enough to answer your patients' questions. Most practices need a homepage, a team page, a fees page, a contact page and a dedicated page for each key treatment, such as dental implants, Invisalign or teeth whitening. Location pages help if you serve several towns.

Can we show testimonials and before-and-after photos on our website?

Yes, as long as they are genuine, used with the patient's consent and presented in line with GDC and ASA rules. Our guide to testimonials and before-and-after photos explains what is allowed.

Do you redesign existing dental websites?

Yes. If your current website is slow, hard to use on a phone or not bringing in enquiries, a dental website redesign can keep what works, such as your content and rankings, while fixing what does not. We set up redirects so you keep the search visibility you already have.
