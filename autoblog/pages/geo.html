The shift

Patients are starting their search with AI

A growing share of searches now return an AI-written answer before any normal results. Google shows AI Overviews at the top of many searches, and more people are asking assistants like ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot and Perplexity direct questions such as "who's a good implant dentist near me?" or "how much is Invisalign in Sheffield?".

Those answers usually name only a handful of practices, and the patient often never sees a list of ten blue links. If your practice isn't one of the names the AI is confident about, you can be invisible for exactly the high-intent questions that lead to bookings, even if you rank well in normal search.

GEO is how you improve those odds. It isn't a trick or a hack. It's making sure the facts about your practice are clear, consistent and backed up across the web, so AI tools can use them with confidence.
