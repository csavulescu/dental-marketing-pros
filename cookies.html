1. What are cookies and similar technologies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. “Local storage” is a similar technology used to save small pieces of information in your browser. They can be strictly necessary for a site to work, or used for optional purposes such as analytics.

2. What we use

Strictly necessary (always on). When you make a cookie choice on our banner, we store a single value in your browser’s local storage (named dmp_cookie_consent) so we can remember your preference. This is essential, contains no personal data and is not used for tracking.

Analytics (optional, only with your consent). If you accept analytics, we load Vercel Web Analytics and Vercel Speed Insights. These are privacy-friendly and cookieless, they do not store cookies on your device or identify you. They help us understand, in aggregate, how the site is used and how it performs. If you reject, they are not loaded.

Advertising cookies. We do not use advertising or third-party marketing cookies on this site.

Fonts. We use Google Fonts, which your browser requests from Google when the page loads. This does not set cookies on our site.

3. Managing your choice

You can change your preference at any time by clicking Cookie settings (also available in the footer of every page), or by clearing your browser’s storage. You can also block or delete cookies through your browser settings.
