How AI tools decide which Doncaster practices to recommend

AI assistants build their answers from sources they can check against each other: your website, Google Business Profile, the NHS service finder, reviews, directories and local news. Gaps or mismatches between them, such as old opening hours on a directory or a treatment missing from your profile, make an assistant less confident about recommending you.

Fixing those gaps is often the quickest win in Doncaster. With lighter online competition than Sheffield or Leeds, a practice with complete, consistent information can become the one AI tools are most confident naming, both for the city and for the towns and villages around it.

What is GEO for a Doncaster dental practice?

GEO (generative engine optimisation) makes your practice easy for AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Google's AI Overviews, Gemini and Perplexity to understand and recommend when Doncaster patients ask about dentists and treatments.

How much does it cost?

GEO starts from £450 per month, and works well alongside our dental SEO. We'll recommend the right scope after a free audit.

Can you guarantee my practice will be recommended?

No. Nobody controls what AI assistants say, and promising placements would breach advertising guidance. We do the work consistently, track how often you're mentioned for the questions that matter and report honestly.

Do you cover practices outside Doncaster itself?

Yes. We work with practices across the DN postcodes, including Bawtry, Tickhill, Thorne, Hatfield, Armthorpe, Conisbrough and Mexborough, and track the AI questions that matter for each practice's own area.
