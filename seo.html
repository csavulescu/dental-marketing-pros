Questions

Dental SEO, FAQs

How long does dental SEO take to work?

Most practices see meaningful ranking and enquiry gains within three to six months, with quick wins like Google Business Profile improvements often showing sooner. SEO compounds, the longer you invest, the stronger your position becomes.

What exactly do I get for £450 a month?

Content creation, content optimisation, technical fixes, one monthly meeting and a monthly report, every month. We'll agree the priorities together so the work always targets your most valuable treatments and areas.

Is there a contract or lock-in?

No long lock-ins. SEO needs a few months to show its value, so we ask for commitment to the process, but we earn your business with results and transparency, not contracts.

Do you guarantee first-page rankings?

No, and you should be wary of anyone who does. Guaranteed-ranking claims breach advertising guidance. We guarantee the process, the work and honest reporting.

How is this different from a generalist agency?

We only work with dentists. We understand treatment values, patient behaviour and the GDC/ASA rules, so the work is sharper and safer than a generalist learning on your budget.

Can I add Google Ads or a new website too?

Yes. Many practices pair SEO with PPC for fast results, and a conversion-focused website to turn the extra traffic into booked appointments.
